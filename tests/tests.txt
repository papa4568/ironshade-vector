# Tests

## Test 1 - Fresh operator onboarding, first recovery, and persistence [sanity]
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: level 1 profile, starter loadout, field tutorial, fixed-step combat, three weapons, abilities, dodge, ACT objective interaction, safe/deep extraction, first curated recovery, XP/level gain, progression point, local persistence, debrief next-action guidance
Expected: A fresh player can complete the first operation without a blocked objective; debrief guidance surfaces new loot or unspent points without changing rewards; first recovery is sparse and usable; XP never exceeds the current level-16 cap; and persistence survives reload without resetting unrelated state.
